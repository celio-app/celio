About and privacy

What Workprint is, what it keeps, and who can see it.

What Workprint is

Workprint is a scheduling tool for creative teams: animation and film productions, student studios and councils, and small creative groups. It keeps track of who is working on what, what is due next, and who is waiting on whom.

What Workprint stores

Your account

  • Your email address and the display name you choose.
  • Your password. It is stored scrambled (hashed) by our sign-in provider. Nobody, including the people who run Workprint, can read it.

Your projects

  • What you and your team type in: project names, tasks, dates, notes, shots, team member names and emails, invite codes, and funding lists.
  • Who belongs to each project and their role.

What Workprint does not do

  • No ads, and your data is never sold.
  • No tracking or analytics. Your browser only keeps what it needs to keep you signed in and remember your colour scheme.
  • Workprint doesn't store your files. Links to files live wherever you keep them (Drive, OneDrive, a studio server).

Who can see your data

A project can only be seen by the people who belong to it. You join a project when its owner invites you or gives you a code. The database itself enforces this, so one project can never read another project's data.

Project owners can add and remove members, and only the person who created a project can delete it.

Services Workprint uses

Workprint runs on a few outside services. Each only gets what it needs to do its job.

Supabase
The database and sign-in. Your account and project data are stored here.
Vercel
Hosts the website at getworkprint.app.
Resend
Sends account emails, such as confirming your address or resetting your password.
Anthropic
Powers the weekly funding scout. Only a project's funding profile (the type of group, field, location and kinds of funding it wants) is sent. Names, emails and tasks are never sent.
Google Fonts, jsDelivr
Load the fonts and code libraries the page needs to display.

These services may store data on servers outside Canada.

Deleting your data

  • A project's creator can delete the project at any time. This removes its tasks, shots, members and funding lists.
  • To delete your account completely, email the contact below and it will be removed.
